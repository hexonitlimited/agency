PremiumTurf Case Study

Implementing an online booking system that increased appointments by 200%

The Challenge

PremiumTurf had a basic website but no way for customers to book services online. All appointments were handled over the phone, which created bottlenecks during peak seasons. They were missing after-hours inquiries and losing potential customers who preferred online booking.

"During spring, our phone would be ringing off the hook. We were missing calls and losing business because customers couldn't book online at their convenience."

- Sarah Williams, Owner of PremiumTurf

The Solution

We developed a comprehensive booking solution for PremiumTurf:

  • Custom online booking system integrated with their website
  • Mobile-responsive design optimized for on-the-go booking
  • Automated confirmation and reminder emails
  • Calendar synchronization with their existing scheduling
  • Service customization with clear pricing options

Timeline: 4 weeks including integration and testing

Package: Growth Bundle with booking system add-on

25+ bookings/month
