Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our one-time pricing packages

What's included in the one-time price?

The one-time price includes design, development, and setup of all features listed in your chosen package. You own the final product and there are no recurring fees for the website itself.

Are there any ongoing costs?

The only ongoing costs would be for website hosting (typically $10-15/month) and domain registration (around $15/year). We can help you set these up with reliable providers.

How long does it take to complete my website?

Most projects are completed within 2-3 weeks depending on the package complexity and how quickly we receive your content. We'll provide a specific timeline after our initial consultation.

What if I need changes after my website is completed?

We offer optional maintenance plans for ongoing updates, or you can make changes yourself since you'll have full access to your website. We also provide training on how to manage basic content updates.
